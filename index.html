      Privacy note

      • Opt-in only. Only people who enroll with consent can be recognised. Everyone else shows as "Unknown" and nothing about them is saved.
      • No photos are stored. Each person is saved as a name plus a "face code": a list of 1,024 numbers that cannot be turned back into a picture.
      • Stays on this device. Face codes and check-ins are kept in this browser only. Nothing is sent to the internet. The camera image is never saved or uploaded.
      • Removal. Anyone can ask to be removed; their entry is deleted with one tap under "People & data". "Delete ALL data" erases everything.
      • Under 18. A parent or guardian must agree before a student is enrolled, and the school must approve the use of this tool.
      • Accuracy. Recognition can make mistakes (lighting, angle, look-alikes). Check-ins should be confirmable and never used alone for discipline or grading.
      • Retention. Delete the data at the end of the event, term, or school year, or when it's no longer needed.
