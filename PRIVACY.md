# Face Attendance: Privacy Note

**What it does.** Face Attendance recognises people who have chosen to enroll so their attendance can be recorded automatically.

**Opt-in only.** Only people who enroll with consent can be recognised. Anyone else appears as "Unknown" and nothing about them is saved.

**What is stored.**
- The person's name.
- A "face code": a list of 1,024 numbers that describes the face. It **cannot be turned back into a picture**.
- Check-in times.

**What is NOT stored.** No photos or video. The camera image is processed live and discarded.

**Where it is stored.** Only in the web browser of the tablet used for attendance. Nothing is sent to the internet or any server. A backup file is only created if staff choose "Export backup", and it must be kept securely.

**Who can see it.** Only people with access to the attendance tablet.

**Removal.** Anyone (or their parent/guardian) can ask to be removed at any time; staff delete the entry with one tap. "Delete ALL data" erases everything on the device.

**Retention.** Data is deleted at the end of the school year or event, or when no longer needed, whichever comes first.

**Accuracy.** Recognition can make mistakes (lighting, angle, look-alikes). Attendance can always be corrected by a teacher and is never used on its own for discipline or grading.

**Under 18.** A parent or guardian must give written consent before a student is enrolled.
