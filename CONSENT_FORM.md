Our class would like to use **Face Attendance**, an app that records attendance by recognising students' faces on a school tablet.

- Only a name and a numeric "face code" are stored, **not photos**.
- Data stays on the school tablet and is **not uploaded** to the internet.
- Taking part is **voluntary**. Students who don't take part are marked present the usual way, with no disadvantage.
- You can withdraw consent at any time and the student's data will be deleted.
- Data is deleted at the end of the school year.
